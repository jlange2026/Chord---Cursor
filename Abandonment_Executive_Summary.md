## 🎯 **KEY INSIGHTS**

### **Callers ARE Engaged**
- **8.8 average turns** before abandonment
- **62.2% abandon LATE** in conversation (after 6+ turns)
- **Not an engagement problem - it's a process problem**

### **The Verification Crisis**
- **230 late-stage verification abandonments** = Callers tried but got frustrated
- **109 early-stage verification abandonments** = Immediate barriers
- **Combined 54%** of abandonments are in identification phase (verification + data collection)

---

## 💡 **QUICK FIXES** *(Immediate Impact)*

### **1. Streamline Verification** 🔧
- Simplify DOB collection process
- Reduce verification attempts (max 2 tries)
- Add progress indicators ("Let me look that up")

### **2. Progressive Data Collection** 📋  
- Collect information as needed, not all upfront
- Make some data optional ("or provide when you arrive")
- Explain why information is needed

### **3. Clarify Intent Discovery** 🤔
- Lead with common options ("scheduling an appointment?") 
- Accept broader responses
- Reduce choice paralysis

---

## 📈 **POTENTIAL IMPACT**

- **457 fewer abandoned calls** (50% reduction in verification + data collection drops)
- **Abandonment rate**: 38.9% → 28.4% (**-10.5% improvement**)
- **Success rate**: 8.4% → 13.9% (**+5.5% improvement**)
